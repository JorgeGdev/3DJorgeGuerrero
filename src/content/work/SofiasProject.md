<h3>Revolutionary Digital Sports Journalism</h3>
Meet Sofia, the most innovative AI-powered sports video generation system that combines cutting-edge artificial intelligence with realistic virtual presenters. This comprehensive platform automatically generates professional sports videos featuring Sofia, a digital Colombian sports journalist who breaks barriers in the traditionally male-dominated world of sports journalism.

<h3>Advanced AI Automation</h3>
Sofia leverages state-of-the-art technologies including OpenAI GPT-4 for intelligent script generation, ElevenLabs for realistic Latin voice synthesis, and Hedra AI for creating stunning videos with 9 different virtual presenters. The system features a complete RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architecture that scrapes and processes sports news from 4 countries: Italy, England, Spain, and France.

<h3>Complete Enterprise Solution</h3>
Built with Node.js and Express, Sofia includes a robust authentication system with JWT tokens, user role management, and a comprehensive admin panel. The platform features real-time dashboards with Server-Sent Events for live monitoring, integrated Telegram bot for automation, and a sophisticated video management system with download capabilities. Users can generate content through simple commands like "sofia3@Barcelona news" and manage the entire workflow through an intuitive glassmorphism-designed interface.

<h3>Professional Content Creation</h3>
The system produces HD videos in 9:16 vertical format, perfect for social media platforms. With 9 virtual presenters available (Sofia 1-9), each video features realistic Spanish narration optimized for Latin audiences. The platform includes manual approval systems, automatic generation capabilities, and real-time statistics tracking, making it ideal for content creators, sports channels, and social media managers.

<h3>Technology Stack & Innovation</h3>
Sofia represents a masterpiece of modern web development, featuring over 7,000 lines of code, 15 integrated dependencies, and 5 major API integrations. The system combines OpenAI's latest GPT-4 model with ElevenLabs voice synthesis, Hedra AI video generation, Supabase database with vector storage, and Telegram Bot API for seamless automation. Deployed on Railway with enterprise-level security measures.

<h3>Impact & Future Vision</h3>
This project showcases the future of automated content creation in sports journalism. Sofia demonstrates how AI can revolutionize digital media production, enabling 24/7 content generation without manual intervention. The platform serves as a productivity tool for content creators, offering multilingual support and the potential for expanding into other sports and languages, representing a significant leap forward in automated video production technology.
